Lighting installation in Orleans.

Lighting quotes for an Orleans home, such as kitchen task and under-cabinet lighting, can look simple on paper yet differ on who supplies fixtures and who repairs the wall or ceiling. Check those two points first.

Plan the light and the work that makes it possible

A lighting project may involve fixture replacement, new wiring points or a different control layout. The contractor needs to assess existing wiring, mounting support, equipment suitability and access. Fixture appearance alone does not establish compatibility with a dimmer or the suitability of a location. Share product information and the intended room use before installation is priced.

Prepare the scope for your Orleans property

Sketch the kitchen with the cabinet runs, the work surfaces and the spots where light is needed. Photograph the ceiling and the underside of the wall cabinets, and collect the product sheets for any fixture already chosen.

Ask whether each quote includes the fixtures or only their installation, where new cable would run to reach the cabinets and who patches and paints any opening. Ask whether the dimmer or switch choice changes the work.

What should the assessment establish?

Before agreeing to a quote for task and under-cabinet lighting in an Orleans kitchen, you need a scope that traces each light to its power source and judges the existing wiring and boxes against the chosen fixtures. Any dimmer is checked against those fixtures, since appearance alone proves nothing. The supplier of each fixture is named, as are the cable path to the cabinets and the finishing left to others. Any ceiling or wall opening is listed, or the scope states that none is needed. Where two quotes list different openings, have the contractor explain why the route differs.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Does a quote for under-cabinet lights include the dimmer?

Not always. A dimmer may be listed separately, and some fixtures only work with certain controls. Ask each contractor to name the control, state who supplies it and confirm the fixture instructions allow it.

Why does one lighting quote mention ceiling repairs and another not?

In a two-storey home with a floor above, reaching a ceiling may mean cutting an opening or fishing cable from another direction. A quote that is silent on this may be leaving the repair to you.
